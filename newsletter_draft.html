KOSKI Service Wins Finland's Digital Innovation Award 2026

KOSKI, a national service for collecting and centralizing information on education and qualifications, has won the 'Vuoden Digiteko 2026' award in the public administration category. The service, part of the Opintopolku platform, benefits over 4.8 million users by streamlining the verification of educational credentials and qualifications. It was praised for its innovative data integration and efficient handling of administrative tasks. KOSKI has also gained international attention, being presented to global organizations like ITU and UNESCO, where it was highlighted as a model for other countries.

Finland's Education Authority Proposes Flexible Qualifications to Support Lifelong Learning

In September 2026, Finland's National Agency for Education (Opetushallitus) convened stakeholders to explore the future of vocational education and the development of qualification systems. The discussion centered on creating more flexible and responsive qualifications that support continuous learning, particularly in growing sectors and for unemployed individuals. The proposed reforms emphasize the role of competence units and modular qualifications in enabling lifelong learning and adapting to rapidly changing workplace needs. The initiative highlights the importance of collaboration between education providers, employers, and policymakers to ensure the education system remains relevant and adaptable in the face of technological and labor market shifts.

Teachers Highlight Interaction, Motivation, and Support as Key to Strengthening Basic Skills

A survey conducted by Opetushallitus (National Board of Education) reveals that Finnish educators prioritize interaction, motivation, and sufficient learning support as key factors in strengthening basic skills. Over 22% of respondents identified enhancing student interaction, participation, and motivation as the most important method for promoting basic skills development, with early childhood education and vocational training teachers highlighting it most. Sufficient learning support came second, with 21% of respondents emphasizing its importance. The findings align with recent policy discussions and highlight the need for systemic changes in how learning support is provided. Educators also stress the importance of digital tools in both supporting and challenging learning, while emphasizing the need for balanced use and guidance to ensure positive outcomes.
