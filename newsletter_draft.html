Application on the basis of open university studies

Applicants can apply for degree programs at HAMK based on completed studies from a Finnish open higher education institution. For a Bachelor’s degree, 60 credits are required, while for a Master’s degree, applicants must have a relevant background, two years of post-degree work experience, and 10 or 15 credits at a Finnish open higher education institution.

HAMK Häme University of Applied Sciences

The HAMK Häme University of Applied Sciences has developed a low-power data logger using an ESP32-C3 Super Mini microcontroller and a DS1308 RTC. This device synchronizes its sampling to UTC and is designed for a sampling period of at least 20 seconds, logging data to both ThingSpeak and flash memory, with the capability to read the flash in web server mode.

HAMK UAS

This summer, residents of small cottages can enjoy a refreshing activity right on their property with the help of HAMK University of Applied Sciences. The project, part of the ILPI initiative funded by the European Union, explores various climate-friendly measures that cottage owners can implement and discusses how these benefits can be extended to the broader community through collective efforts.
